Single-Sided Non-contact Laser–EMAT Imaging of Deep Submillimetre Defects单侧非接触 Laser–EMAT 深层亚毫米缺陷成像

Can a single-sided non-contact system locate deeply embedded submillimetre defects in thick metallic components?能否用单侧非接触系统定位厚壁金属构件中的深层亚毫米缺陷？

Motivation研究动机
Deep targets produce weak echoes, and a finite receiver aperture and offset source–receiver geometry change how those echoes are recorded.深层目标回波较弱，有限接收孔径和偏置收发几何会改变回波的记录方式。
Methodology研究方法
I model the offset source–receiver geometry and receiver aperture, then combine delay-and-sum focusing with coherence weighting.对偏置收发几何和接收孔径建模，结合延时叠加聚焦与相干加权。

My contribution个人贡献
I developed and integrated the test platform, modelled the acquisition geometry and implemented finite-aperture-aware coherent imaging.搭建并集成实验平台，建立收发几何模型，实现有限孔径相干成像。
Key findings主要结果
The study uses separate dense-scanning and coarse-step single-pulse experiments. Each tests a different aspect of localisation and acquisition efficiency.研究分别采用密集扫查和粗步距单脉冲实验，分别检验定位表现与采集效率。

Physics-Informed Reconstruction of Sparse Laser-Ultrasonic Wavefields物理信息神经网络与稀疏激光超声波场重建

Can physically constrained learning reconstruct wavefields from sparse, low-SNR measurements?能否利用物理约束学习从稀疏、低信噪比测量中恢复波场？

Motivation研究动机
High-density reference scans are time-consuming. Reconstruction from fewer measurements can make wavefield analysis more practical.高密度参考扫查耗时较长，降低采样需求有助于波场分析的实际应用。
Methodology研究方法
A Kirchhoff–Love thin-plate equation constrains a neural network that reconstructs A0-dominated flexural wavefields. PDE residuals provide a scattering-sensitive defect indicator.以 Kirchhoff–Love 薄板方程约束神经网络，重建 A0 模态主导的弯曲波场，并以偏微分方程残差构建对散射敏感的缺陷指示。

Non-contact 2.5-D Topography Measurement of Rough Additively Manufactured Surfaces粗糙增材制造表面的非接触 2.5-D 形貌测量

How can air-coupled laser ultrasonics recover local topography on rough additively manufactured surfaces?如何利用空耦激光超声重建粗糙增材制造表面的局部形貌？

Motivation研究动机
Geometric bias in time-of-flight maps can obscure the surface relief that needs to be measured.到时图中的几何偏差会掩盖待测表面起伏。
Methodology研究方法
A bias-compensation model combines calibrated time-of-flight residuals and echo amplitude to estimate local surface height or depth.通过偏差补偿将标定后的飞行时间残差与回波幅值结合，估计局部表面高度或深度。

My contribution个人贡献
First-listed author of the IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement study.IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement 论文列名第一。
Key findings主要结果
In line-scan tests, compensation reduced the spatial standard deviation of time of flight from 5.19 to 0.150 μs. The effective lateral resolution was 65.2 ± 4.3 μm under the tested conditions.直线扫查中，补偿将飞行时间的空间标准差从 5.19 μs 降至 0.150 μs。测试条件下有效横向分辨率为 65.2 ± 4.3 μm。

Motivation研究动机
Severe solid–air impedance mismatch makes weak interfacial signals especially difficult to interpret.严重固–气声阻抗失配使弱界面信号难以判读。

Decoupling Propagation and Texture-Induced Artifacts in Laser Ultrasonics激光超声传播波场与织构诱导伪影解耦

Can propagating wavefronts be separated from texture-locked artefacts in measurements of woven CFRP?能否将编织 CFRP 测量中的传播波前与跟随织构的伪影分开？

Motivation研究动机
Woven microstructure can obscure arrival times and bias directional velocity estimates.编织微结构会干扰到时提取，并使方向波速估计产生偏差。
Methodology研究方法
A structural prior derived from measured wavefields guides the separation of the propagating and texture-associated components.以实测波场提取的结构先验，引导传播分量与织构相关分量的分离。

Single-Pulse Continuous-Motion Ultrasonic Inspection单脉冲连续运动超声检测

How can a moving specimen be scanned with one A-scan per laser pulse while retaining useful localisation information?如何在试件连续运动条件下实现每个激光脉冲采集一条 A-scan，并保留有用的定位信息？

Motivation研究动机
Stop-and-acquire scanning limits throughput. Continuous motion requires careful position assignment and motion-aware processing.停走式采集限制检测效率。连续运动条件下需要合理分配采集位置并考虑运动对成像的影响。
Methodology研究方法
The stage drives the specimen at constant speed. The laser fires at a fixed repetition rate and each pulse yields one A-scan. The scan proceeds without stopping or pulse averaging.运动平台带动试件匀速运动，激光以固定重复频率发射，每脉冲采集一条 A-scan，扫查过程中不停顿、不平均。
