Physics-informed understanding.非接触测量，

I am a PhD student at Wuhan University. I develop non-contact laser-ultrasonic measurements for composite interfaces and additively manufactured materials, combining experimental systems, multiphysics modelling and physics-informed learning.我是武汉大学博士研究生，面向复合材料界面与增材制造材料，结合实验系统、多物理场建模和物理约束学习，开展非接触激光超声测量与检测研究。

My work connects how signals are acquired, how waves interact with materials, and how measurements can reliably reveal defects, material properties or manufacturing-process changes.我的工作连接信号采集、波与材料的相互作用，以及如何从测量中可靠地提取缺陷、材料特性和制造过程变化信息。

I combine laser-ultrasonic experiments with Kirchhoff–Love physics-informed learning to recover flexural wavefields and derive scattering-sensitive defect indicators.结合激光超声实验与 Kirchhoff–Love 物理约束学习，恢复弯曲波场，并构建对缺陷散射敏感的指示量。

Separating wave propagation from composite texture分离超声传播与复合材料纹理干扰

My role: 个人贡献：First author · Conceptualization, methodology, software, data curation and writing.第一作者 · 研究构思、方法、软件、数据整理与论文撰写。

Sparse wavefield reconstruction and defect indication in low-SNR laser ultrasonics using physics-informed neural networks

Baoding Wang†, Jiuzhang Li†, Haodong Chen, Liequan Wu, Pan Ma, Bin Yang, Jun Zhang

Ultrasonics 168, 108194 (2026).

Co-first author共同第一作者DOI ↗Code ↗代码 ↗
Summary & my contribution研究概述与个人贡献

A Kirchhoff–Love plate-constrained PINN reconstructs A0-dominated wavefields from sparse, low-SNR measurements. Aluminum-plate experiments use 20–30% spatial samples and three signal averages, compared with 300-average reference scans. PDE residuals provide a scattering-sensitive defect indicator rather than a direct defect-geometry reconstruction.利用 Kirchhoff–Love 薄板方程约束 PINN，在低信噪比、稀疏采样条件下重建 A0 主导波场。铝板实验使用 20–30% 空间采样和 3 次平均，与 300 次平均参考扫描比较；PDE 残差用于散射敏感的缺陷指示，而非直接重建缺陷几何形状。

My contribution: 个人贡献：Conceptualization, software, validation, formal analysis, data curation, visualization and original-draft writing; equal contribution with Jiuzhang Li.研究构思、软件、验证、分析、数据整理、可视化与初稿撰写；与 Jiuzhang Li 共同贡献。
